# Business Decisions Required Register

The following Service Quality rules must be explicitly defined by the Business before the Rating Platform can be activated:
1. **Rating Scale:** (e.g. 1-5 Stars, Thumbs Up/Down)
2. **Review Length Constraints:** Maximum characters allowed in free-text reviews.
3. **Rating Window Expiry:** Can a customer rate a ride from 2 years ago?
4. **Rating Editing/Deletion:** Are ratings immutable once submitted?
5. **Quality Enforcement Threshold:** Is there an automatic suspension threshold (e.g. banning Partners below 4.2 stars)?

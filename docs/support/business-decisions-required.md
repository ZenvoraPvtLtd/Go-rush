# Business Decisions Required Register

The following Support policies must be explicitly defined by the Business before the Case Management platform can be utilized for Live Operations:
1. **Case Taxonomy:** Explicit Categories (Ride, Payment, Safety).
2. **SLA Targets:** Expected response times (e.g. 24h for Payment, 1h for Safety).
3. **Refund Policy:** Rules authorizing agents to issue compensation.
4. **Partner Penalties:** Rules governing suspension/deactivation for valid complaints.
5. **Reopening Window:** Number of days a Customer has to reopen a Resolved case before it is permanently Closed.

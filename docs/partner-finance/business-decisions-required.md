# Business Decisions Required Register

The following financial rules must be provided by the business before the Partner Ledger can be activated:
1. **Commission Percentage:** Flat rate, tiered, or distance-based?
2. **Minimum Payout Threshold:** (e.g. £50.00 / ₹1000)
3. **Settlement Frequency:** Daily, Weekly, or On-Demand?
4. **Payout Gateway:** Stripe Connect, RazorpayX, or other?
5. **Tax Withholding:** GST/TDS requirements for payouts.
